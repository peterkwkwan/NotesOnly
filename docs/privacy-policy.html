NotesOnly

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026 · Package: com.notes.notes_only

Who we are

NotesOnly is a solo-practitioner SOAP notes CRM for physiotherapists, operated by Peter Kwan (“we”, “us”). The Android app is published under package name com.notes.notes_only. This policy explains how NotesOnly handles personal and clinical data. It is written with Hong Kong’s Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (PDPO) in mind, in plain language.

What data we process

Depending on how you use the app, NotesOnly may store:

  • Practitioner identity — your Google account name, email, and authentication identifiers used to sign in.
  • Client personal data — names, contact details, and related client records you enter.
  • Appointments — scheduling information you create.
  • SOAP / clinical notes — health-related clinical content you write (sensitive health data).
  • Practice settings — configuration such as practice name and app preferences.

You decide what client and clinical information to enter. NotesOnly is a tool for your practice; you remain responsible for having a lawful basis to record and use your clients’ personal and health data.

Why we process data

We process data only to:

  • Provide the NotesOnly app on your device.
  • Optionally back up and sync your practice data across your signed-in devices.
  • Authenticate you securely with Google Sign-In.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use your data for advertising.

Local storage

NotesOnly is local-first. Practice data is stored on your device in a local SQLite database (Drift). The app works with data on the device even when cloud sync is not used.

Google Sign-In and Firebase

Optional cloud sync uses Firebase Authentication (Google Sign-In only) and Cloud Firestore in Firebase project notesonly-e4829. When you sign in and sync is enabled, your practice data is transmitted to and stored in Google Firebase so you can back up and restore it.

Google processes authentication and cloud storage as our service provider. See Google’s privacy documentation for Firebase and Google Sign-In for how Google handles data in those products.

Health and clinical data

SOAP notes and related clinical fields are sensitive health information. They are stored on your device and, if you use sync, in your Firebase-backed account storage. Treat device access and your Google account security with care. We do not share clinical notes with third parties for marketing or analytics.

Ads and analytics

NotesOnly does not include advertising SDKs. We do not add separate analytics SDKs beyond what Firebase Authentication and Firestore require to provide those services.

Retention

Data retention is under your control. Local data remains on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app (which removes on-device data subject to your device’s storage behaviour). Cloud-synced data remains in your Firebase-linked account until you delete that data or your account access is removed through the product’s account-deletion path. After account deletion requests are completed, associated cloud copies are removed from our Firebase project subject to normal backup and propagation delays.

Third parties

The main third-party processors involved in optional sync and sign-in are Google (Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore). We do not sell or rent your data to other companies.

Children’s privacy

NotesOnly is intended for adult healthcare practitioners. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 for use of the app as an end user.

Hong Kong / PDPO

If you practise in Hong Kong, you are generally the data user for client records you enter. NotesOnly helps you store and (optionally) sync those records. We aim to keep processing limited to what is needed to run the app and optional backup/sync, to keep data reasonably secure, and to respond to reasonable access or deletion requests about the operator-controlled account data we hold.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of NotesOnly after an update means you accept the revised policy.
